Practical energy information

Use electricity more efficiently

Simple habits can reduce avoidable energy use and help households and businesses plan their electricity needs.

💡

Lighting

Use efficient LED lamps, switch off lights in unused spaces and make use of daylight where practical.

❄️

Refrigeration

Keep refrigerator doors closed, allow ventilation around the unit, check door seals and avoid placing hot food directly inside.

🔌

Appliance choices

Check appliance energy labels and compare power ratings before purchase. Unplug idle equipment where safe and appropriate.

📊

Plan usage

List essential appliances, estimate their daily hours and prioritise critical loads when planning a backup system.

🏫

Schools

Switch off unused classroom equipment and plan computer, lighting and practical-lab use around available power.

🏪

Small businesses

Identify equipment that must run continuously, especially refrigeration, and seek professional advice on reliable backup power.

Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Can I request electrical, solar or CCTV services?

Yes. You can submit a service request through the website. V. UMOREN MULTI SERVICES LTD will review your requirements and contact you to discuss the next steps.

How are technicians selected and assigned?

Our company manages technician applications, profile publication and job assignments. Customers communicate with the company rather than hiring technicians directly.

Can customers pay technicians directly?

No. Customer payments for company-managed services are handled by V. UMOREN MULTI SERVICES LTD. Payment arrangements and quotations are communicated by the company.

Are equipment prices live market prices?

No. Supplier prices and equipment availability are confirmed during quotation. The website does not currently retrieve live supplier prices.

Are technician profiles automatically verified?

No. Profiles are prepared and managed by our company. Any qualifications or credentials are described according to the checks actually completed. Publication requires the technician's consent.

Are the learning resources official examination materials?

No. They are introductory learning resources and are not official WAEC or NECO examination papers or publications.

Can I track a submitted request?

Yes. Use the reference code and private tracking details provided after submission to check the recorded status.

Can I use the website on a phone?

Yes. The website is designed to adapt to mobile screens.

About our platform

Connecting Services, Skills and Opportunities

VUMOREN CONNECT – Smart Energy & Education is a digital platform developed by V. UMOREN MULTI SERVICES LTD to connect households, businesses, professionals and learners with practical services, procurement opportunities and accessible technical learning.

Our Mission

To make reliable technical services, equipment procurement, practical learning and professional opportunities more accessible to individuals, businesses and communities.

Our Service Model

Our company coordinates customer enquiries, quotations, service delivery, payments and technician assignments. Customers deal directly with the company throughout the service process.

Professional Opportunities

Qualified technicians and service providers can apply to join our professional network. Approved and consenting professionals may have public profiles displaying relevant skills and experience.

Learning and Community Impact

We aim to support practical electrical, solar and CCTV learning, energy awareness, vocational development and wider access to useful information.
