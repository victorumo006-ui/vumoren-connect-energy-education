About the initiative

Local innovation for digital inclusion

VUMOREN CONNECT – Smart Energy & Education is a proposed digital inclusion initiative by V. Umoren Multi Services Ltd. It aims to make practical energy information and learning support more accessible to underserved communities.

Development status: This is an early demonstration prototype. The solar estimator and downloadable sample guides work in the browser. Enquiry submission, technician matching, live pricing, user accounts and cloud-based learning services are not yet connected.
