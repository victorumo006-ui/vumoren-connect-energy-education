Practical energy information

Use electricity more efficiently

Simple habits can reduce avoidable energy use and help households and businesses plan their electricity needs.

💡

Lighting

Use efficient LED lamps, switch off lights in unused spaces and make use of daylight where practical.

❄️

Refrigeration

Keep refrigerator doors closed, allow ventilation around the unit, check door seals and avoid placing hot food directly inside.

🔌

Appliance choices

Check appliance energy labels and compare power ratings before purchase. Unplug idle equipment where safe and appropriate.

📊

Plan usage

List essential appliances, estimate their daily hours and prioritise critical loads when planning a backup system.

🏫

Schools

Switch off unused classroom equipment and plan computer, lighting and practical-lab use around available power.

🏪

Small businesses

Identify equipment that must run continuously, especially refrigeration, and seek professional advice on reliable backup power.

Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Is the solar assessment a final system design?

No. It is an educational estimate based on the figures entered. A qualified professional must inspect the site and confirm the design, equipment ratings and safety requirements.

Are the equipment prices live market prices?

No. The budget tool adds quotation amounts entered by the user. It does not retrieve supplier prices or guarantee equipment availability.

Can I currently book a technician through this website?

Not yet. Technician registration, verification, directory listings and customer matching are planned features. The current form only demonstrates how an enquiry could be collected.

Are the learning resources official examination materials?

No. They are introductory resources prepared for general learning. They are not official WAEC or NECO papers or publications.

Can I use the website on a phone?

Yes. The page is designed to adapt to mobile screens. Downloadable text resources can be saved for offline reading after download.

About the initiative

Local innovation for digital inclusion

VUMOREN CONNECT – Smart Energy & Education is an initiative by V. Umoren Multi Services Ltd. It aims to make practical energy information, service connections and learning support more accessible to underserved communities.

Intended community impact

Support better-informed energy decisions, encourage efficient electricity use, improve access to introductory technical learning and create a pathway toward trusted local service connections.

Development approach

Start with accessible digital tools and learning content, then develop secure request handling, provider verification, partnerships and community feedback processes.

Current prototype status: Solar assessment, quotation-based budget calculation, downloadable learning resources, quiz feedback and demo enquiry confirmation run in the browser. Enquiry processing, technician verification and matching, live pricing, user accounts and structured instructor-led courses are not yet connected or operational.
